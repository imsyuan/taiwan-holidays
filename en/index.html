FAQ

What are Taiwan's public holidays in 2027?

Taiwan's official 2027 calendar lists 24 holiday entries and 8 long weekends of 3 or more days. Open the 2027 page for the full list with dates.

When is Chinese (Lunar) New Year 2027 in Taiwan?

The 2027 Lunar New Year holiday in Taiwan runs from Thursday, February 4, 2027 to Wednesday, February 10, 2027, 7 days in a row.

Does Taiwan have make-up workdays in 2027?

Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2027. Since 2026, the official government calendar no longer includes make-up workdays; when a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives a compensatory day off on a nearby weekday instead.

How do I plan time off around Taiwan's holidays?

Each long-weekend page shows up to three leave plans that link the break to nearby weekends, and the year page has a table of the best plan per holiday. The same engine is available to AI agents through the MCP server.

Is there a free Taiwan holidays API?

Yes. Static JSON files need no API key: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/imsyuan/taiwan-holidays/data/<year>/holidays-en.json (public holidays) and https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/imsyuan/taiwan-holidays/data/<year>/calendar-en.json (full-year calendar).
