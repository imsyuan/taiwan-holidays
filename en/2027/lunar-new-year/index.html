Lunar New Year holiday 2027: Thursday, February 4, 2027 to Wednesday, February 10, 2027 (7 days)

Taiwan's Lunar New Year holiday 2027 runs from Feb 4 (Thu) to Feb 10 (Wed): 7 days off in a row (2 weekend days plus 5 weekday holidays: Lunar New Year's Eve Eve, Lunar New Year's Eve, Lunar New Year, Compensatory Leave). It is the longest break of 2027. Best way to extend it: take 2 days off (Feb 11 (Thu), Feb 12 (Fri)) for 11 days in a row, Feb 4 (Thu) to Feb 14 (Sun).

How to extend the Lunar New Year holiday 2027: 3 leave plans

Plans 1 and 2 come from this site's workday engine (same as the MCP optimize_leave): take working days off before or after the break to link it to another weekend or holiday. Plan 3 combines both sides.

Plan 1

Take 2 days off, get 11 days in a row (5.5 days of break per day of leave)

Leave days: Feb 11 (Thu), Feb 12 (Fri)

Break: Feb 4 (Thu) to Feb 14 (Sun), 11 days in total: 2 leave days, the other 9 are the Lunar New Year holiday, weekends or other days off.

Plan 2

Take 3 days off, get 12 days in a row (4 days of break per day of leave)

Leave days: Feb 1 (Mon), Feb 2 (Tue), Feb 3 (Wed)

Break: Jan 30 (Sat) to Feb 10 (Wed), 12 days in total: 3 leave days, the other 9 are the Lunar New Year holiday, weekends or other days off.

Plan 3 (Plans 1 + 2 combined: leave before and after)

Take 5 days off, get 16 days in a row (3.2 days of break per day of leave)

Leave days: Feb 1 (Mon), Feb 2 (Tue), Feb 3 (Wed), Feb 11 (Thu), Feb 12 (Fri)

Break: Jan 30 (Sat) to Feb 14 (Sun), 16 days in total: 5 leave days, the other 11 are the Lunar New Year holiday, weekends or other days off.

FAQ

When is the Lunar New Year holiday in Taiwan in 2027?

The 2027 Lunar New Year holiday runs from Thursday, February 4, 2027 to Wednesday, February 10, 2027, 7 days in a row: 2 weekend days and 5 weekday holidays (Lunar New Year's Eve Eve, Lunar New Year's Eve, Lunar New Year, Compensatory Leave).

How many days of leave do I need to extend the Lunar New Year holiday 2027?

Most efficient: take 2 days off (Feb 11 (Thu), Feb 12 (Fri)) for 11 days in a row, Feb 4 (Thu) to Feb 14 (Sun). Longest break: take 5 days off (Feb 1 (Mon), Feb 2 (Tue), Feb 3 (Wed), Feb 11 (Thu), Feb 12 (Fri)) for 16 days in a row, Jan 30 (Sat) to Feb 14 (Sun).

Do I have to work a make-up day around the Lunar New Year holiday 2027?

No. Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2027, so the weeks around the Lunar New Year holiday follow the normal Monday-to-Friday schedule.

Why does the Lunar New Year holiday 2027 include a compensatory day off?

2 days in this break are compensatory leave (Feb 9 (Tue), Feb 10 (Wed)). When a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives an extra day off on a nearby weekday, and it is an official day off.

What is the next long weekend in Taiwan after the Lunar New Year holiday 2027?

After the Lunar New Year holiday, the next long weekend in 2027 is the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend (Saturday, February 27, 2027 to Monday, March 1, 2027, 3 days), 16 days later.
