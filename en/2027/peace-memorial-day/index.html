Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend 2027: Saturday, February 27, 2027 to Monday, March 1, 2027 (3 days)

Taiwan's Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend 2027 runs from Feb 27 (Sat) to Mar 1 (Mon): 3 days off in a row (2 weekend days plus 1 weekday holidays: Peace Memorial Day, Compensatory Leave). It is number 3 by length among the 8 long weekends of 2027 (tied with 5 others). Best way to extend it: take 4 days off (Mar 2 (Tue), Mar 3 (Wed), Mar 4 (Thu), Mar 5 (Fri)) for 9 days in a row, Feb 27 (Sat) to Mar 7 (Sun).

FAQ

When is the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend in Taiwan in 2027?

The 2027 Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend runs from Saturday, February 27, 2027 to Monday, March 1, 2027, 3 days in a row: 2 weekend days and 1 weekday holidays (Peace Memorial Day, Compensatory Leave).

How many days of leave do I need to extend the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend 2027?

Most efficient: take 4 days off (Mar 2 (Tue), Mar 3 (Wed), Mar 4 (Thu), Mar 5 (Fri)) for 9 days in a row, Feb 27 (Sat) to Mar 7 (Sun). Longest break: take 9 days off (Feb 22 (Mon), Feb 23 (Tue), Feb 24 (Wed), Feb 25 (Thu), Feb 26 (Fri), Mar 2 (Tue), Mar 3 (Wed), Mar 4 (Thu), Mar 5 (Fri)) for 16 days in a row, Feb 20 (Sat) to Mar 7 (Sun).

Do I have to work a make-up day around the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend 2027?

No. Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2027, so the weeks around the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend follow the normal Monday-to-Friday schedule.

Why does the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend 2027 include a compensatory day off?

1 day in this break is compensatory leave (Mar 1 (Mon)). When a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives an extra day off on a nearby weekday, and it is an official day off.

What is the next long weekend in Taiwan after the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend 2027?

After the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend, the next long weekend in 2027 is the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend (Saturday, April 3, 2027 to Tuesday, April 6, 2027, 4 days), 32 days later.
