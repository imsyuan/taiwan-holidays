Taiwan Public Holidays 2027: Long Weekends and Leave Planner

Taiwan's 2027 government office calendar has 365 days: 121 non-working days (weekends included) and 244 working days. The official calendar lists 24 holiday entries (including 8 compensatory days off and 7 holidays that fall on weekends) and 8 long weekends of 3 or more days. The longest break is the Lunar New Year holiday (Feb 4 (Thu) to Feb 10 (Wed), 7 days in a row). Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2027. Since 2026, the official government calendar no longer includes make-up workdays; when a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives a compensatory day off on a nearby weekday instead.

Make-up workdays in Taiwan, 2027

Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2027. Since 2026, the official government calendar no longer includes make-up workdays; when a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives a compensatory day off on a nearby weekday instead.

FAQ

How many public holidays does Taiwan have in 2027?

The official calendar lists 24 holiday entries for 2027, including 8 compensatory days off and 7 holidays that fall on a Saturday or Sunday. Of the year's 365 days, 121 are non-working days (weekends included) and 244 are working days.

When is the Lunar New Year holiday in Taiwan in 2027?

The 2027 Lunar New Year holiday runs from Thursday, February 4, 2027 to Wednesday, February 10, 2027, 7 days in a row, covering Lunar New Year's Eve Eve, Lunar New Year's Eve, Lunar New Year, Compensatory Leave and 2 weekend days.

What is the longest holiday break in Taiwan in 2027?

The longest stretch in 2027 is the Lunar New Year holiday: Thursday, February 4, 2027 to Wednesday, February 10, 2027, 7 days. Taking 5 days of leave around it extends the break to 16 days.

Does Taiwan have make-up workdays in 2027?

Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2027. Since 2026, the official government calendar no longer includes make-up workdays; when a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives a compensatory day off on a nearby weekday instead.

Which days should I take off in 2027 to get the longest break?

The most efficient plan for 2027: take 2 days off (Feb 11 (Thu), Feb 12 (Fri)) for 11 days in a row, Feb 4 (Thu) to Feb 14 (Sun). Runner-up: take 3 days off (Feb 1 (Mon), Feb 2 (Tue), Feb 3 (Wed)) for 12 days in a row, Jan 30 (Sat) to Feb 10 (Wed).

How many long weekends (3+ days) does Taiwan have in 2027?

8 long weekends of 3 or more consecutive days off (weekends included): New Year's Day long weekend Jan 1–Jan 3 (3 days); Lunar New Year holiday Feb 4–Feb 10 (7 days); Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend Feb 27–Mar 1 (3 days); Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend Apr 3–Apr 6 (4 days); Labor Day long weekend Apr 30–May 2 (3 days); National Day (Double Ten) long weekend Oct 9–Oct 11 (3 days); Taiwan Retrocession Day long weekend Oct 23–Oct 25 (3 days); Constitution Day long weekend Dec 24–Dec 26 (3 days).

Where does the 2027 Taiwan holiday data come from, and can I use it in code?

It comes from the Government Office Calendar published by the DGPA, Executive Yuan, on data.gov.tw (dataset 14718), synced monthly. English JSON: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/imsyuan/taiwan-holidays/data/2027/holidays-en.json (public holidays) and https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/imsyuan/taiwan-holidays/data/2027/calendar-en.json (full-year calendar). AI agents can use the MCP server https://mcp.tw-holidays.gooliya.com/mcp to count workdays and plan leave.

Free data and API for 2027

All figures come from these JSON files (free, no API key; dates are YYYYMMDD):

  • holidays-en.json – 2027 public holidays in English (CDN: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/imsyuan/taiwan-holidays/data/2027/holidays-en.json)
  • calendar-en.json – full-year daily calendar in English (CDN: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/imsyuan/taiwan-holidays/data/2027/calendar-en.json)
  • makeup-workdays.json – make-up workdays

For AI agents, the MCP server (https://mcp.tw-holidays.gooliya.com/mcp, Streamable HTTP, no auth) provides count_workdays, add_workdays, next_makeup_workday, optimize_leave and find_travel_windows.
