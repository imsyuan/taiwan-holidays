FAQ

When is the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend in Taiwan in 2027?

The 2027 Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend runs from Saturday, April 3, 2027 to Tuesday, April 6, 2027, 4 days in a row: 2 weekend days and 2 weekday holidays (Children's Day, Tomb Sweeping Day, Compensatory Leave).

How many days of leave do I need to extend the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend 2027?

Most efficient: take 3 days off (Apr 7 (Wed), Apr 8 (Thu), Apr 9 (Fri)) for 9 days in a row, Apr 3 (Sat) to Apr 11 (Sun). Longest break: take 8 days off (Mar 29 (Mon), Mar 30 (Tue), Mar 31 (Wed), Apr 1 (Thu), Apr 2 (Fri), Apr 7 (Wed), Apr 8 (Thu), Apr 9 (Fri)) for 16 days in a row, Mar 27 (Sat) to Apr 11 (Sun).

Do I have to work a make-up day around the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend 2027?

No. Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2027, so the weeks around the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend follow the normal Monday-to-Friday schedule.

Why does the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend 2027 include a compensatory day off?

1 day in this break is compensatory leave (Apr 6 (Tue)). When a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives an extra day off on a nearby weekday, and it is an official day off.

What is the next long weekend in Taiwan after the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend 2027?

After the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend, the next long weekend in 2027 is the Labor Day long weekend (Friday, April 30, 2027 to Sunday, May 2, 2027, 3 days), 23 days later.
