New Year's Day long weekend 2027: Friday, January 1, 2027 to Sunday, January 3, 2027 (3 days)

Taiwan's New Year's Day long weekend 2027 runs from Jan 1 (Fri) to Jan 3 (Sun): 3 days off in a row (2 weekend days plus 1 weekday holidays: New Year's Day). It is number 3 by length among the 8 long weekends of 2027 (tied with 5 others). Best way to extend it: take 5 days off (Jan 4 (Mon), Jan 5 (Tue), Jan 6 (Wed), Jan 7 (Thu), Jan 8 (Fri)) for 10 days in a row, Jan 1 (Fri) to Jan 10 (Sun).

FAQ

When is the New Year's Day long weekend in Taiwan in 2027?

The 2027 New Year's Day long weekend runs from Friday, January 1, 2027 to Sunday, January 3, 2027, 3 days in a row: 2 weekend days and 1 weekday holidays (New Year's Day).

How many days of leave do I need to extend the New Year's Day long weekend 2027?

Most efficient: take 5 days off (Jan 4 (Mon), Jan 5 (Tue), Jan 6 (Wed), Jan 7 (Thu), Jan 8 (Fri)) for 10 days in a row, Jan 1 (Fri) to Jan 10 (Sun).

Do I have to work a make-up day around the New Year's Day long weekend 2027?

No. Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2027, so the weeks around the New Year's Day long weekend follow the normal Monday-to-Friday schedule.

What is the next long weekend in Taiwan after the New Year's Day long weekend 2027?

After the New Year's Day long weekend, the next long weekend in 2027 is the Lunar New Year holiday (Thursday, February 4, 2027 to Wednesday, February 10, 2027, 7 days), 31 days later.
