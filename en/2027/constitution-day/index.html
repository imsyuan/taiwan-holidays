Constitution Day long weekend 2027: Friday, December 24, 2027 to Sunday, December 26, 2027 (3 days)

Taiwan's Constitution Day long weekend 2027 runs from Dec 24 (Fri) to Dec 26 (Sun): 3 days off in a row (2 weekend days plus 1 weekday holidays: Compensatory Leave, Constitution Day). It is number 3 by length among the 8 long weekends of 2027 (tied with 5 others). Best way to extend it: take 4 days off (Dec 20 (Mon), Dec 21 (Tue), Dec 22 (Wed), Dec 23 (Thu)) for 9 days in a row, Dec 18 (Sat) to Dec 26 (Sun).

FAQ

When is the Constitution Day long weekend in Taiwan in 2027?

The 2027 Constitution Day long weekend runs from Friday, December 24, 2027 to Sunday, December 26, 2027, 3 days in a row: 2 weekend days and 1 weekday holidays (Compensatory Leave, Constitution Day).

How many days of leave do I need to extend the Constitution Day long weekend 2027?

Most efficient: take 4 days off (Dec 20 (Mon), Dec 21 (Tue), Dec 22 (Wed), Dec 23 (Thu)) for 9 days in a row, Dec 18 (Sat) to Dec 26 (Sun). Longest break: take 8 days off (Dec 20 (Mon), Dec 21 (Tue), Dec 22 (Wed), Dec 23 (Thu), Dec 27 (Mon), Dec 28 (Tue), Dec 29 (Wed), Dec 30 (Thu)) for 14 days in a row, Dec 18 (Sat) to Dec 31 (Fri).

Do I have to work a make-up day around the Constitution Day long weekend 2027?

No. Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2027, so the weeks around the Constitution Day long weekend follow the normal Monday-to-Friday schedule.

Why does the Constitution Day long weekend 2027 include a compensatory day off?

1 day in this break is compensatory leave (Dec 24 (Fri)). When a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives an extra day off on a nearby weekday, and it is an official day off.

What is the next long weekend in Taiwan after the Constitution Day long weekend 2027?

The Constitution Day long weekend is the last long weekend of 3 or more days in 2027. The previous one is the Taiwan Retrocession Day long weekend (Oct 23–Oct 25, 3 days).
