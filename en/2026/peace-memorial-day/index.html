FAQ

When is the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend in Taiwan in 2026?

The 2026 Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend runs from Friday, February 27, 2026 to Sunday, March 1, 2026, 3 days in a row: 2 weekend days and 1 weekday holidays (Compensatory Leave, Peace Memorial Day).

How many days of leave do I need to extend the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend 2026?

Most efficient: take 4 days off (Feb 23 (Mon), Feb 24 (Tue), Feb 25 (Wed), Feb 26 (Thu)) for 16 days in a row, Feb 14 (Sat) to Mar 1 (Sun). Longest break: take 9 days off (Feb 23 (Mon), Feb 24 (Tue), Feb 25 (Wed), Feb 26 (Thu), Mar 2 (Mon), Mar 3 (Tue), Mar 4 (Wed), Mar 5 (Thu), Mar 6 (Fri)) for 23 days in a row, Feb 14 (Sat) to Mar 8 (Sun).

Do I have to work a make-up day around the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend 2026?

No. Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2026, so the weeks around the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend follow the normal Monday-to-Friday schedule.

Why does the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend 2026 include a compensatory day off?

1 day in this break is compensatory leave (Feb 27 (Fri)). When a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives an extra day off on a nearby weekday, and it is an official day off.

What is the next long weekend in Taiwan after the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend 2026?

After the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend, the next long weekend in 2026 is the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend (Friday, April 3, 2026 to Monday, April 6, 2026, 4 days), 32 days later.
