FAQ

Can I download or print the Taiwan 2026 calendar for free?

Yes. Download the A4 portrait PNG (300 dpi) or press 'Print / Save as PDF' to export a PDF. The data is released under CC BY 4.0.

Are there make-up workdays in Taiwan in 2026?

No. Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2026, so no weekend day is converted into a working day.

Where does the holiday data come from?

It comes from the Government Office Calendar published by the DGPA, Executive Yuan, via data.gov.tw (dataset 14718), synced monthly. Official announcements always prevail.

Source: Taiwan government open data portal, DGPA Executive Yuan "Government Office Calendar" (dataset 14718), synced monthly. Official announcements prevail.
