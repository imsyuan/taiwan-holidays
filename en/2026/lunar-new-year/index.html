Lunar New Year holiday 2026: Saturday, February 14, 2026 to Sunday, February 22, 2026 (9 days)

Taiwan's Lunar New Year holiday 2026 runs from Feb 14 (Sat) to Feb 22 (Sun): 9 days off in a row (4 weekend days plus 5 weekday holidays: Lunar New Year's Eve Eve, Lunar New Year's Eve, Lunar New Year, Compensatory Leave). It is the longest break of 2026. Best way to extend it: take 4 days off (Feb 23 (Mon), Feb 24 (Tue), Feb 25 (Wed), Feb 26 (Thu)) for 16 days in a row, Feb 14 (Sat) to Mar 1 (Sun).

FAQ

When is the Lunar New Year holiday in Taiwan in 2026?

The 2026 Lunar New Year holiday runs from Saturday, February 14, 2026 to Sunday, February 22, 2026, 9 days in a row: 4 weekend days and 5 weekday holidays (Lunar New Year's Eve Eve, Lunar New Year's Eve, Lunar New Year, Compensatory Leave).

How many days of leave do I need to extend the Lunar New Year holiday 2026?

Most efficient: take 4 days off (Feb 23 (Mon), Feb 24 (Tue), Feb 25 (Wed), Feb 26 (Thu)) for 16 days in a row, Feb 14 (Sat) to Mar 1 (Sun). Longest break: take 9 days off (Feb 9 (Mon), Feb 10 (Tue), Feb 11 (Wed), Feb 12 (Thu), Feb 13 (Fri), Feb 23 (Mon), Feb 24 (Tue), Feb 25 (Wed), Feb 26 (Thu)) for 23 days in a row, Feb 7 (Sat) to Mar 1 (Sun).

Do I have to work a make-up day around the Lunar New Year holiday 2026?

No. Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2026, so the weeks around the Lunar New Year holiday follow the normal Monday-to-Friday schedule.

Why does the Lunar New Year holiday 2026 include a compensatory day off?

1 day in this break is compensatory leave (Feb 20 (Fri)). When a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives an extra day off on a nearby weekday, and it is an official day off.

What is the next long weekend in Taiwan after the Lunar New Year holiday 2026?

After the Lunar New Year holiday, the next long weekend in 2026 is the Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend (Friday, February 27, 2026 to Sunday, March 1, 2026, 3 days), 4 days later.
