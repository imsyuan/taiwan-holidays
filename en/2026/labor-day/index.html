Labor Day long weekend 2026: Friday, May 1, 2026 to Sunday, May 3, 2026 (3 days)

Taiwan's Labor Day long weekend 2026 runs from May 1 (Fri) to May 3 (Sun): 3 days off in a row (2 weekend days plus 1 weekday holidays: Labor Day). It is number 4 by length among the 9 long weekends of 2026 (tied with 5 others). Best way to extend it: take 4 days off (Apr 27 (Mon), Apr 28 (Tue), Apr 29 (Wed), Apr 30 (Thu)) for 9 days in a row, Apr 25 (Sat) to May 3 (Sun).

FAQ

When is the Labor Day long weekend in Taiwan in 2026?

The 2026 Labor Day long weekend runs from Friday, May 1, 2026 to Sunday, May 3, 2026, 3 days in a row: 2 weekend days and 1 weekday holidays (Labor Day).

How many days of leave do I need to extend the Labor Day long weekend 2026?

Most efficient: take 4 days off (Apr 27 (Mon), Apr 28 (Tue), Apr 29 (Wed), Apr 30 (Thu)) for 9 days in a row, Apr 25 (Sat) to May 3 (Sun). Longest break: take 9 days off (Apr 27 (Mon), Apr 28 (Tue), Apr 29 (Wed), Apr 30 (Thu), May 4 (Mon), May 5 (Tue), May 6 (Wed), May 7 (Thu), May 8 (Fri)) for 16 days in a row, Apr 25 (Sat) to May 10 (Sun).

Do I have to work a make-up day around the Labor Day long weekend 2026?

No. Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2026, so the weeks around the Labor Day long weekend follow the normal Monday-to-Friday schedule.
