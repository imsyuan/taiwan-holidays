Mid-Autumn Festival long weekend 2026: Friday, September 25, 2026 to Monday, September 28, 2026 (4 days)

Taiwan's Mid-Autumn Festival long weekend 2026 runs from Sep 25 (Fri) to Sep 28 (Mon): 4 days off in a row (2 weekend days plus 2 weekday holidays: Mid-Autumn Festival, Confucius Birthday (Teachers' Day)). It is number 2 by length among the 9 long weekends of 2026 (tied with 1 other). Best way to extend it: take 4 days off (Sep 21 (Mon), Sep 22 (Tue), Sep 23 (Wed), Sep 24 (Thu)) for 10 days in a row, Sep 19 (Sat) to Sep 28 (Mon).

FAQ

When is the Mid-Autumn Festival long weekend in Taiwan in 2026?

The 2026 Mid-Autumn Festival long weekend runs from Friday, September 25, 2026 to Monday, September 28, 2026, 4 days in a row: 2 weekend days and 2 weekday holidays (Mid-Autumn Festival, Confucius Birthday (Teachers' Day)).

How many days of leave do I need to extend the Mid-Autumn Festival long weekend 2026?

Most efficient: take 4 days off (Sep 21 (Mon), Sep 22 (Tue), Sep 23 (Wed), Sep 24 (Thu)) for 10 days in a row, Sep 19 (Sat) to Sep 28 (Mon). Longest break: take 8 days off (Sep 21 (Mon), Sep 22 (Tue), Sep 23 (Wed), Sep 24 (Thu), Sep 29 (Tue), Sep 30 (Wed), Oct 1 (Thu), Oct 2 (Fri)) for 16 days in a row, Sep 19 (Sat) to Oct 4 (Sun).

Do I have to work a make-up day around the Mid-Autumn Festival long weekend 2026?

No. Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2026, so the weeks around the Mid-Autumn Festival long weekend follow the normal Monday-to-Friday schedule.

What is the next long weekend in Taiwan after the Mid-Autumn Festival long weekend 2026?

After the Mid-Autumn Festival long weekend, the next long weekend in 2026 is the National Day (Double Ten) long weekend (Friday, October 9, 2026 to Sunday, October 11, 2026, 3 days), 10 days later.
