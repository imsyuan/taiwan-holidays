National Day (Double Ten) long weekend 2026: Friday, October 9, 2026 to Sunday, October 11, 2026 (3 days)

Taiwan's National Day (Double Ten) long weekend 2026 runs from Oct 9 (Fri) to Oct 11 (Sun): 3 days off in a row (2 weekend days plus 1 weekday holidays: Compensatory Leave, National Day). It is number 4 by length among the 9 long weekends of 2026 (tied with 5 others). Best way to extend it: take 4 days off (Oct 5 (Mon), Oct 6 (Tue), Oct 7 (Wed), Oct 8 (Thu)) for 9 days in a row, Oct 3 (Sat) to Oct 11 (Sun).

FAQ

When is the National Day (Double Ten) long weekend in Taiwan in 2026?

The 2026 National Day (Double Ten) long weekend runs from Friday, October 9, 2026 to Sunday, October 11, 2026, 3 days in a row: 2 weekend days and 1 weekday holidays (Compensatory Leave, National Day).

How many days of leave do I need to extend the National Day (Double Ten) long weekend 2026?

Most efficient: take 4 days off (Oct 5 (Mon), Oct 6 (Tue), Oct 7 (Wed), Oct 8 (Thu)) for 9 days in a row, Oct 3 (Sat) to Oct 11 (Sun). Longest break: take 9 days off (Oct 5 (Mon), Oct 6 (Tue), Oct 7 (Wed), Oct 8 (Thu), Oct 12 (Mon), Oct 13 (Tue), Oct 14 (Wed), Oct 15 (Thu), Oct 16 (Fri)) for 16 days in a row, Oct 3 (Sat) to Oct 18 (Sun).

Do I have to work a make-up day around the National Day (Double Ten) long weekend 2026?

No. Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2026, so the weeks around the National Day (Double Ten) long weekend follow the normal Monday-to-Friday schedule.

Why does the National Day (Double Ten) long weekend 2026 include a compensatory day off?

1 day in this break is compensatory leave (Oct 9 (Fri)). When a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives an extra day off on a nearby weekday, and it is an official day off.

What is the next long weekend in Taiwan after the National Day (Double Ten) long weekend 2026?

After the National Day (Double Ten) long weekend, the next long weekend in 2026 is the Taiwan Retrocession Day long weekend (Saturday, October 24, 2026 to Monday, October 26, 2026, 3 days), 12 days later.
