Taiwan Public Holidays 2026: Long Weekends and Leave Planner

Taiwan's 2026 government office calendar has 365 days: 120 non-working days (weekends included) and 245 working days. The official calendar lists 22 holiday entries (including 6 compensatory days off and 6 holidays that fall on weekends) and 9 long weekends of 3 or more days. The longest break is the Lunar New Year holiday (Feb 14 (Sat) to Feb 22 (Sun), 9 days in a row). Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2026. Since 2026, the official government calendar no longer includes make-up workdays; when a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives a compensatory day off on a nearby weekday instead.

Make-up workdays in Taiwan, 2026

Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2026. Since 2026, the official government calendar no longer includes make-up workdays; when a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives a compensatory day off on a nearby weekday instead.

FAQ

How many public holidays does Taiwan have in 2026?

The official calendar lists 22 holiday entries for 2026, including 6 compensatory days off and 6 holidays that fall on a Saturday or Sunday. Of the year's 365 days, 120 are non-working days (weekends included) and 245 are working days.

When is the Lunar New Year holiday in Taiwan in 2026?

The 2026 Lunar New Year holiday runs from Saturday, February 14, 2026 to Sunday, February 22, 2026, 9 days in a row, covering Lunar New Year's Eve Eve, Lunar New Year's Eve, Lunar New Year, Compensatory Leave and 4 weekend days.

What is the longest holiday break in Taiwan in 2026?

The longest stretch in 2026 is the Lunar New Year holiday: Saturday, February 14, 2026 to Sunday, February 22, 2026, 9 days. Taking 9 days of leave around it extends the break to 23 days.

Does Taiwan have make-up workdays in 2026?

Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2026. Since 2026, the official government calendar no longer includes make-up workdays; when a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives a compensatory day off on a nearby weekday instead.

How many long weekends (3+ days) does Taiwan have in 2026?

9 long weekends of 3 or more consecutive days off (weekends included): Lunar New Year holiday Feb 14–Feb 22 (9 days); Peace Memorial Day (228) long weekend Feb 27–Mar 1 (3 days); Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend Apr 3–Apr 6 (4 days); Labor Day long weekend May 1–May 3 (3 days); Dragon Boat Festival long weekend Jun 19–Jun 21 (3 days); Mid-Autumn Festival long weekend Sep 25–Sep 28 (4 days); National Day (Double Ten) long weekend Oct 9–Oct 11 (3 days); Taiwan Retrocession Day long weekend Oct 24–Oct 26 (3 days); Constitution Day long weekend Dec 25–Dec 27 (3 days).

Where does the 2026 Taiwan holiday data come from, and can I use it in code?

It comes from the Government Office Calendar published by the DGPA, Executive Yuan, on data.gov.tw (dataset 14718), synced monthly. English JSON: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/imsyuan/taiwan-holidays/data/2026/holidays-en.json (public holidays) and https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/imsyuan/taiwan-holidays/data/2026/calendar-en.json (full-year calendar). AI agents can use the MCP server https://mcp.tw-holidays.gooliya.com/mcp to count workdays and plan leave.

Free data and API for 2026

All figures come from these JSON files (free, no API key; dates are YYYYMMDD):

  • holidays-en.json – 2026 public holidays in English (CDN: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/imsyuan/taiwan-holidays/data/2026/holidays-en.json)
  • calendar-en.json – full-year daily calendar in English (CDN: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/imsyuan/taiwan-holidays/data/2026/calendar-en.json)
  • makeup-workdays.json – make-up workdays

For AI agents, the MCP server (https://mcp.tw-holidays.gooliya.com/mcp, Streamable HTTP, no auth) provides count_workdays, add_workdays, next_makeup_workday, optimize_leave and find_travel_windows.
