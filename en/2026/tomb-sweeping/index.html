FAQ

When is the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend in Taiwan in 2026?

The 2026 Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend runs from Friday, April 3, 2026 to Monday, April 6, 2026, 4 days in a row: 2 weekend days and 2 weekday holidays (Compensatory Leave, Children's Day, Tomb Sweeping Day).

How many days of leave do I need to extend the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend 2026?

Most efficient: take 4 days off (Mar 30 (Mon), Mar 31 (Tue), Apr 1 (Wed), Apr 2 (Thu)) for 10 days in a row, Mar 28 (Sat) to Apr 6 (Mon). Longest break: take 8 days off (Mar 30 (Mon), Mar 31 (Tue), Apr 1 (Wed), Apr 2 (Thu), Apr 7 (Tue), Apr 8 (Wed), Apr 9 (Thu), Apr 10 (Fri)) for 16 days in a row, Mar 28 (Sat) to Apr 12 (Sun).

Do I have to work a make-up day around the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend 2026?

No. Taiwan has no make-up workdays in 2026, so the weeks around the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend follow the normal Monday-to-Friday schedule.

Why does the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend 2026 include a compensatory day off?

2 days in this break are compensatory leave (Apr 3 (Fri), Apr 6 (Mon)). When a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the government gives an extra day off on a nearby weekday, and it is an official day off.

What is the next long weekend in Taiwan after the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend 2026?

After the Tomb Sweeping Day (Qingming) long weekend, the next long weekend in 2026 is the Labor Day long weekend (Friday, May 1, 2026 to Sunday, May 3, 2026, 3 days), 24 days later.
