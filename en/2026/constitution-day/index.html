Constitution Day long weekend 2026: Friday, December 25, 2026 to Sunday, December 27, 2026 (3 days)

Taiwan's Constitution Day long weekend 2026 runs from Dec 25 (Fri) to Dec 27 (Sun): 3 days off in a row (2 weekend days plus 1 weekday holidays: Constitution Day). It is number 4 by length among the 9 long weekends of 2026 (tied with 5 others). Best way to extend it: take 4 days off (Dec 21 (Mon), Dec 22 (Tue), Dec 23 (Wed), Dec 24 (Thu)) for 9 days in a row, Dec 19 (Sat) to Dec 27 (Sun).

FAQ

When is the Constitution Day long weekend in Taiwan in 2026?

The 2026 Constitution Day long weekend runs from Friday, December 25, 2026 to Sunday, December 27, 2026, 3 days in a row: 2 weekend days and 1 weekday holidays (Constitution Day).

Do I have to work a make-up day around the Constitution Day long weekend 2026?
